    ⚠ THIS ROW WIDENS `allowed_numbers`. It carries 97, 3, 30 and 40. `3` and `30` are

    more thing the outbound guard will permit the model to say. Say the word and I drop the
    two statistics — the row still answers the question without «97%» and «30-40».

Үс унах, хуурайших, хагарах зэрэг асуудалтай бол мастер үсчинд үзүүлж, зөвлөгөө авахыг

    PLACED AS KNOWLEDGE, NOT AS A CANNED ROW, and the difference matters. A canned row
    pointed at by a rule is SERVED — the platform types it. Knowledge is read by the model
    and followed or not, which D-065 calls a request rather than an instruction.

    Enforcing it would need a matcher for "the customer is describing damaged hair", and
    that is a fuzzy judgement no stem list makes well — «гэмтсэн», «хуурай», «унаж байна»

    guidance now, and becomes a served row later if the corpus shows the model ignoring it.
