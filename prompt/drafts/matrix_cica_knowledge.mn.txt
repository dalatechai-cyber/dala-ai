A. knowledge_documents — «CICA нөхөн сэргээх эмчилгээ»

Гурван үе шаттай эмчилгээ.
Үсний уураг, чийгийг нөхөн сэргээнэ. Үс 97% уураг, 3% ус агуулдаг.
Үсний меланин пигментийг нөхнө.
Нэг удаагийн эмчилгээ нь 30-40 тэжээлийн тостой дүйцэхүйц үр дүнтэй. Энэ нь тэжээлийн
тос биш бөгөөд түүнээс хамаагүй илүү үр дүнтэй.
Шулуутгах химийн бодис агуулсан уургийн эмчилгээнээс илүү үр дүнтэй.
Үс унах, хуурайших, хагарах, гэмтэх тохиолдолд тохиромжтой.

    ⚠ THIS ROW WIDENS `allowed_numbers` BY EXACTLY TWO TOKENS: `97` and `30-40`.
    MEASURED 2026-09-20, not reasoned — `allowedNumbersFrom` run over this exact prose
    returns ["3","30-40","97"], and `3` is already on Matrix's live list (seq 8, thirteen
    tokens). The earlier guess in this file — that `40` would be new and `30-40` "may
    compile as one fused token" — was wrong on both halves: the hyphenated form IS one
    token, so a bare `40` is never licensed.

    Neither new token can license a price, and that is a property of the comparison rather
    than of the numbers: `extractNumerals` reduces to digits only and the guard compares
    reduced forms, so `30-40` reduces to `3040` and licenses nothing else, and `97` licenses
    `97` alone — not `97,000`. Matrix's prices are five and six digits.

    So the cost is precisely: the bot may say «97» or «30-40» in some unrelated sentence.
    Say the word and I drop the two statistics — the row still answers the question without
    «97%» and «30-40».

C. The damaged-hair standard answer
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Үс унах, хуурайших, хагарах зэрэг асуудалтай бол мастер үсчинд үзүүлж зөвлөгөө аваарай.

    PLACED AS KNOWLEDGE, NOT AS A CANNED ROW, and the difference matters. A canned row
    pointed at by a rule is SERVED — the platform types it. Knowledge is read by the model
    and followed or not, which D-065 calls a request rather than an instruction.

    Enforcing it would need a matcher for "the customer is describing damaged hair", and
    that is a fuzzy judgement no stem list makes well — «гэмтсэн», «хуурай», «унаж байна»
    over-match and under-match in the ways D-067 and D-083 both catalogue. So it goes in as
    guidance now, and becomes a served row later if the corpus shows the model ignoring it.
