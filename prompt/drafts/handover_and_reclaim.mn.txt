────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. HANDOVER NOTICE — the bot has passed the thread to a person
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option A (no timing promise — RECOMMENDED):

    Уучлаарай, би энэ асуултад хариулж чадахгүй байна. Манай ажилтанд шилжүүллээ,
    тэд энд хариулна.

    "Sorry, I can't answer this question. I've passed it to our staff; they will
    reply here."

    «энд» — HERE — is the load-bearing word, and the whole difference from today's
    line, which ends at 7741-7777. It promises no time, so it cannot be broken by a
    salon that is busy.

Option B (with a timing promise):

    Уучлаарай, би энэ асуултад хариулж чадахгүй байна. Манай ажилтанд шилжүүллээ,
    удахгүй энд хариулна.

    …«удахгүй» means *shortly*. Warmer, and a promise the salon has already failed to keep
    on the telephone. If the reclaim window is short, B is defensible; if it is not, B
    is the phone line again in a new place.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. RECLAIM — nobody picked the thread up, the bot is coming back
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option A (does not blame the salon — RECOMMENDED):

    Хүлээлгэсэнд уучлаарай. Би үргэлжлүүлэн туслая — асуултаа бичвэл хариулна.

    "Sorry for the wait. Let me keep helping — write your question and I'll answer."

    Says nothing about why. The customer does not need to be told the salon did not
    look, and the salon should not be told on its own Page.

Option B (explicit):

    Хүлээлгэсэнд уучлаарай. Манай ажилтан одоогоор хариулж амжаагүй байна.
    Би үргэлжлүүлэн туслая — асуултаа бичвэл хариулна.

    "…Our staff haven't managed to reply yet…" — honest, longer, and it reports a
    business's own response time to its customer.
