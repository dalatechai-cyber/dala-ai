1. handover_notice — sent WITH the pass, when the bot hands the thread to a person
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Хамт олон маань энэ яриаг аван тантай холбогдоно. Тэр хүртэл би хариулахгүй,
түр хүлээнэ үү.

    "Our team will take this conversation and get in touch with you. Until then I
     will not reply — please wait a moment."

Why it is worded this way:

  — It EXPLAINS THE SILENCE that follows, which is the whole reason for sending it
    («Silence with no explanation is what makes people give up»). A pass with no notice
    and a dead channel look identical from the customer's side.
  — It promises no TIME. «удахгүй», «хэдхэн минутын дотор» and anything like them are a
    commitment the salon has to keep, and D-069's «Хаяг» lesson is that a label promising
    more than it delivers is worse than declining.
  — «би хариулахгүй» is first person and explicit. The customer is told the bot is
    stepping back, not that it broke.
  — It does NOT say «Уучлаарай». The bot is not apologising for fetching a person; that
    is the system working.

2. handover_reclaim — sent when the window expires and nobody came
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Уучлаарай, хүлээлгэсэнд. Би үргэлжлүүлэн туслая. Хүнтэй ярих шаардлагатай бол
хэлээрэй.

    "Sorry for keeping you waiting. Let me carry on helping you. If you need to
     speak to a person, say so."

Your sentence, kept — «Уучлаарай, хүлээлгэсэнд. Би үргэлжлүүлэн туслая.» is grammatical
and lands correctly: «хүлээлгэсэн» is the causative, so the bot apologises for HAVING MADE
the customer wait rather than for the customer's own waiting.

Two notes on what was added and what was refused:

  — THE THIRD SENTENCE IS DELIBERATE and breaks the source prompt's «нэг, ихдээ хоёр
    өгүүлбэр» rule. This is the one moment the customer has evidence that asking for a
    person does not work, so the door has to be held open explicitly. Drop it if you would
    rather keep the two-sentence rule; the reply is still honest without it.
  — NO REASON IS GIVEN. «Хамт олон маань завгүй байна» — *our team is busy* — was drafted
    and rejected: nobody was necessarily busy, they may simply not have seen it, and the
    platform cannot tell the difference. Asserting a cause we cannot observe is the same
    failure as a label promising an address.
