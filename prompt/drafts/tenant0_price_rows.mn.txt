WHY B1–B8 ARE HELD — measured 2026-09-19, by execution
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. «эхо» and «ора» are THREE code points and MIN_STEM_CHARS is 4, so B3 and B5 are
   skipped with `stem_too_short` and can never fire. Two of the five staff would be
   silently unanswerable. `stem_sequence` would get past the floor but is a
   `gate/match.ts` mode; `deterministic_replies.match_mode` permits only
   `whole_message | contains_stem`.

2. `matchDeterministic` returns the FIRST matching rule and has no ambiguity verdict.
   «Дали Вира хоёрыг авбал үнэ хэд вэ?» matches B1 and B2; measured, the same rules in
   the opposite array order answer with the other price. `reception/load.ts` selects
   `deterministic_replies` with no `order by`, so which price a customer is quoted is
   not merely first-wins — it is unspecified.

   That is the failure D-075 exists to prevent, rebuilt in a different table: a real,
   allow-listed price attached to a product the customer did not ask about. Here it is
   worse than in the prefix, because this path sends the row VERBATIM with no model and
   no outbound guard in the loop.

3. `requires_empty_history` defaults TRUE, and a price question arrives mid-conversation.
   Every B row needs it set false explicitly. (Row A too.)

(3) is a column value. (1) and (2) are the table's and the matcher's, and the fix is
small: give `matchDeterministic` an ambiguity verdict — when two enabled rules match one
message, fire NEITHER and let the model answer. That is the module's own stated doctrine
(«every ambiguity here resolves towards the model»), it costs one model call, and
`deterministic_replies` currently holds ZERO rows for either tenant, so nothing in
production can change behaviour under it.
