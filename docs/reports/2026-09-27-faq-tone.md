# DalaTech FAQ answers: proposed rewording (2026-09-27)

The founder asked, after the live test at publish seq 10, for every FAQ answer to be
checked for curtness. A FAQ answer reaches customers verbatim: when the model's reply
adapts it, `gate/pinned.ts` serves the row's own bytes instead. **Approved and applied
2026-09-27 (D-150):** rows 1–4 as proposed; payment terms in the founder's own wording
(«…сарын төлбөрийг сар бүрийн 5-ны дотор QPay эсвэл дансаар төлнө.»).

The four answers the founder wrote (discount, annual prepay, minimum contract, VAT) are
already live in `faqs` (D-149). The FAQ that says «Сарын төлбөрт … багтана» and the one about a
custom quote read as full sentences, so they are left as they are.
