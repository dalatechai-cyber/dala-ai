# DalaTech FAQ answers: proposed rewording (2026-09-27)

The founder asked, after the live test at publish seq 10, for every FAQ answer to be
checked for curtness. A FAQ answer reaches customers verbatim: when the model's reply
adapts it, `gate/pinned.ts` serves the row's own bytes instead. Nothing below has been
changed; each line waits for the founder's approval.

The four answers the founder wrote (discount, annual prepay, minimum contract, VAT) are
already live in `faqs` (D-149). The FAQ that says «Сарын төлбөрт … багтана» and the one about a
custom quote read as full sentences, so they are left as they are.

AI ажилтны суурилуулалтыг нэг удаа, сарын төлбөрийг сар бүрийн эхэнд төлнө. |

The model-side rule the founder set (full polite sentences, never a bare «Тийм.», never a
clipped noun phrase, no minus sign on a discount) is drafted in Mongolian as
`prompt/drafts/02_style_full_sentences.mn.txt`. It becomes item (5) of the platform style
block `02_style` once the founder signs it (`prompt/platform-mn-review.json`). It would apply
to every tenant at that tenant's next publish.
