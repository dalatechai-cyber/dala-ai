# 23–24 Sep: 22 Tara Яармаг messages with no recorded answer (2026-09-30)

Read-only investigation. Nothing was changed to find any of this. Customer ids only; no message
text is reproduced here.

## What happened

- **The channel was still in `shadow`.**
  - Tara Яармаг's Page went live on 2026-09-24 at 21:54 UTC, which is 05:54 on 25 Sep in
    Ulaanbaatar (D-118, D-119).
  - On 23 and 24 Sep the old bot (the ancestor, Meta app `1380702870025418`) was still the one
    answering customers.
  - Dala AI only drafted replies in parallel, and sent none by design.
- **Our drafts were all refused by `canned_stale`.**
  - `canned_responses` rows had been edited after the published revision (seq 12, 22 Sep
    00:05 Ulaanbaatar), and nothing republished until seq 13 (25 Sep 00:59 Ulaanbaatar).
  - Every turn therefore stopped at the canned-hash check in `src/lib/reception/handle.ts`,
    before the model. That explains the missing model spend and the empty `answered_by`.
  - QStash retried and gave up on each event. The founder's Telegram received 29
    `webhook.delivery_exhausted` and 8 `webhook.stranded_event` pages between 21 and 24 Sep
    (D-113, "canned_stale ran for two and a half days").
- **Webhook delivery was fine.**
  - Meta delivered every event (`webhook_events` 23 Sep: 76, 24 Sep: 42, all `processed`).
  - The inbound rows were stored. Only our reply step refused.

## Did those customers really get no reply?

customer an echo afterwards.
- The first reply came from the ancestor app every time.
- The slowest was 18 seconds after the customer's message.
- This was measured from the echoes stored in `webhook_events.raw_payload`, on Meta's clock.

## Could it happen again? Yes, and now it would be real

The cause is still possible, and it would now hurt a customer.
- The ancestor has been unsubscribed since the cutover, so there is no second bot left to
  answer.
- Any direct edit to Tara's `canned_responses` rows without a republish puts the tenant back in
  `canned_stale`. From then on every reply 503s, including ones that need no model.
- Today the first sign is a `webhook.delivery_exhausted` page about 3 minutes after the first
  unanswered message. QStash tries once more at about 33 minutes, which is past the 15-minute
  reply limit.
- Since the cutover, `live_exhausted_alerts = 0`, so it has not recurred. All 11 inbound rows
  with no bot answer since go-live were deliberate `human_has_thread` holds (a person had the
  conversation).

**Proposed fix (not built; waiting for approval):**

1. **Detect drift before a customer does.**
   - The hourly health run compares each live tenant's snapshot `canned_hash` with
     `cannedHashOf` of its current rows, using the same function the reply path uses.
   - On a mismatch it pages once (`on_change`, closed when they match again). The page names
     the tenant and says "republish now".
   - It reads only and cannot change a reply.
2. **Optional, stricter:** a trigger that refuses direct `canned_responses` edits for a `live`
   channel unless `reviewed_at` is cleared in the same statement. This would force the
   provisioning path, which already clears the stamp and expects a publish.

   It is a migration touching a live table, so it is the founder's call. Option 1 alone is
   cheap and enough to see it coming.

## A separate finding from the live period: a person holds the chat, and nobody answers

When staff reply from the Page Inbox, the bot steps back (`human_has_thread`) for the takeover
cooldown. Of the 11 live messages the bot deliberately left:

- **9 were answered by staff** (inbox app `263902037430900`) or needed no answer, such as a
  thank-you or "I'll book closer to the date".
- **2 waited a day or more:**
  - Conversation `bdd7d67e-69c8-479d-b6a0-d2607c7dec4f`: a price question on 25 Sep 06:19,
    answered about 33 hours later.
  - Conversation `393f8744-38a1-491c-9641-fa03c797af3d`: a stylist question with a photo on
    29 Sep 08:54, answered by staff about 24.5 hours later.

Both were eventually answered, so no follow-up is needed now. The risk is structural: while a
person holds the conversation the bot stays silent, and nothing checks that the person actually
replied. That is a policy decision (D-080, `docs/handover.md`) and is not changed here.
