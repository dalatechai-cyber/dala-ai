6. **Built and verified:** `scripts/bakeoff/arms.ts` runs Tara's real loader, gate and guards over a dump whose prompt matches live seq 15 byte for byte (`21ac422e…`), and swaps only the model. `compare.mjs` scores your six criteria and prints every reply for your native read. 7. **Egune facts found:** an OpenAI-compatible API; the official SDK names model `egune1-14b`. **No public price was readable**, and third-party code shows replies can carry `<think>` traces (the harness strips and counts them).
8. **Measured already: the upside is small.** Sonnet 5 on Tara costs **$0.0116 per reply** in production (217 calls, 80.6% cache hits), and ~465–651 replies/month reach a model. That is **$5.4–7.6 ≈ ₮19–27k a month**, so even a free Egune saves at most that. At the older 60.5 replies/day the ceiling is ~₮75k.
9. **The permanent tests cannot tell the two models apart:** all 6 of Tara's reply cases, and 22 of the 72 test messages, are answered by deterministic rows before any model is called.
10. **Verdict, pending the run: do not replace Sonnet 5 for Дали.** The saving is capped at ₮19–27k/month (8–11% of the ₮250k price), and D-009 already failed a smaller model (Haiku) on Mongolian fluency. Egune would earn a partial role, such as Latin-typed messages, only if the run shows it beating Sonnet there on your read.
