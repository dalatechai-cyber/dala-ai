They asked for prices three ways and got none. **Turn 2 is worse than a refusal: they
answered the question and we asked it again.** Two later turns in the same conversation
open «Тийм ээ, …боломжтой» and «…шууд орохгүй байх магадлалтай» — substantively
compatible, but a customer reading both sees a bot changing its mind (D-069's shape).

**Why loading the list is not data entry.** `sections.ts` already reads `service_variants`,
`tenant.ts` already renders them as an L3 «ҮНИЙН ЖАГСААЛТ», and `allowedNumbersFrom` runs
over every tenant section. I built the full 43 entries into an intake document and ran it:

```

154,000, 16,500, 176,000, 190,000, 198,000, 19:00, 200,000, 20:00, 22,000, 220,000,

75,000, 76001888, 8,000, 80,000, 80905498, 85,000, 88,000, 99,000
```

**45 tokens, 38 of them prices** (up from today's 13). The guard checks a numeral is *on*
the list, never that it belongs to the service being discussed — so «Омбре 33,000₮», a real
price against a 500,000–640,000 service, passes every check. A real price against the wrong
service is more plausible to a customer than an invented one, which makes it worse.

**In fairness to the mechanism:** the provisioning path is not blind to this. It prints that
list every run under *"Every numeral the bot would be permitted to type. Read it as a list of

null`. You are shown the list and must clear a blocker. But once you confirm the facts — and
you will, the salon gave you the list — the 38 numerals go live.

#### The proposal, all four parts

| # | Part | Customer-visible? |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **`price_list` stops emitting figures.** Renders service *names* only, so the model can still say "yes, we do that" and name it correctly, and cannot quote a number. `allowed_numbers` is never widened by prices. | No |
| **2** | **A pre-model short-circuit serves the price from the row**, as `gate/pinned.ts` serves a reviewed row's bytes. | **3** | **The served text is `{service name} — {price}` plus one reviewed tail sentence.** The label line is a name, digits and punctuation — no grammar — rendered by the *same* `priceOf`/`formatMoney` the publisher uses, so the two cannot disagree. Only the tail sentence is new Mongolian. | **Yes — one sentence** |
| **4** | **`allowed_numbers` and the outbound guard are untouched.** A short-circuit is not model text, so it is not guarded — which is the point. | No |

**Refinement found while auditing, not in last night's version:** `priceOf` has five kinds
and only three carry a numeral. `on_inspection` renders «(үзээд)» and `none` renders
`(none: children_services)` — no figure, and `none` is how the prompt says *this service
exists and its price is deliberately withheld, see this refusal*. `tenant.test.ts` already
asserts it. **Suppress the numbers; keep the kinds.**

**Options**

- **A — Build parts 1+2+4 now, sign the tail sentence when you can (recommended).** Parts
  1, 2 and 4 are mechanism with no Mongolian in them. They can land and sit inert until a
  `confirmed_at` variant exists. Then loading the list is one `apply.ts --apply`.
- **B — Load the list first, fix the renderer after.** Do not. It is the one ordering that
  puts 38 prices in the model's reach with no relational check. A window of hours is a
  window.
- **C — Ship without prices.** What we do today. Measured cost: the 12:19 conversation.

**If you pick wrong:** B risks a real price on the wrong service — the failure D-075 exists
to prevent, now produced by us instead of the model, and the customer has no way to tell.
C's cost is known and bounded: we stay worse than the ancestor on the most common question a
salon gets.

**Effort, corrected down from last night:** loading is `scripts/provision/apply.ts` with a
JSON intake document — it already handles services, variants, aliases and comment rules, with
a dry run by default. I overstated this before. The work is parts 1 and 2, not the data.

## How far from replacing the ancestor, and the shortest path

**Not far in engineering, and further than I said in evidence.** The platform is in better
shape than the bot it replaces: it cannot invent a price, it cannot leak its own prompt
without being caught, every draft carries `revision_id` and `prompt_hash`, refusals are
counted, and this morning it answered a Latin-script booking question with the link in
**one** reply and eighteen seconds — where the ancestor's own construction takes four
(D-042). That is the first measured case of us being *better* rather than merely safe.

But two things are true that were not in yesterday's report. First, **the mirror evidence on
the current config is nine turns, not eighty-seven.** The 87 belong to seq 7; seq 8 was
published at 23:22 last night and has nine inbound turns behind it. My own STATUS banner said
otherwise and was wrong. Second, **the price gap is not theoretical any more** — a customer
this afternoon asked for prices three ways, got none, and was asked a clarifying question
they had already answered.

The shortest path is four things and none of them is long: **settle the four names with the
salon** (one conversation), **build parts 1, 2 and 4 of the price design** (mechanism, no
Mongolian, lands inert), **sign the tail sentence and the CICA rows** (one reading evening),
then **load the list through `apply.ts` and run the mirror two days on the new revision** —
long enough that `revision_id` can answer *did that edit help*, which is the entire reason
those columns exist. The hands-on work in §3 is under an hour. The salon conversation and
the reading evening are the schedule.

Go live when a price question gets a price. Not before.
